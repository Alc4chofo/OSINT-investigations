# OSINT Investigations

A collection of write-ups documenting OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) investigations, mostly into phishing campaigns and other scams that land in my inbox.

The goal is to show the process rather than just the conclusion: what the initial lure looked like, which signals gave it away, what public records were pulled, and where the trail went cold. Each investigation is written so that someone with no prior background can follow the reasoning and apply the same steps themselves.

Everything documented here uses publicly available information only. Findings are reported to the relevant authorities and abuse contacts before or alongside publication.

## Investigations

- [Anatomy of a Phishing Campaign: An Email Targeting My University](Phising/phishing-investigation-article.md) — a fake tuition invoice, an AI-generated PDF, and two compromised school domains.

## On the use of AI

AI tools are used in this repository **only for grammatical correction and structural editing.** English is not my first language, so I use them to fix grammar and tidy up the flow of the writing.

All research, analysis, conclusions, and technical findings are entirely my own. No AI tool performed any part of the investigations, generated any findings, or wrote any of the substantive content.
